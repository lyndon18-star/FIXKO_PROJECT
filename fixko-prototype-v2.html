Trust & verification

Why every faculty account is checked by hand

Students verify with a school email and Student Number. Because faculty accounts see department-wide reports, Fixko asks for one more thing before a teacher account goes live.

What a faculty sign-up requires
  • ✉
    School faculty email
    Must match your school's verified domain — checked automatically at sign-up.
  • #
    Employee / Teacher ID number
    A unique identifier the admin office already has on file — no two accounts can share one.
  • 🪪
    Photo of your school ID
    Uploaded at sign-up and matched against the ID number by an admin before the account is approved.
What happens after you sign up
Submitted
Under review
Verified

Your account is created right away in a pending state so nothing blocks your sign-up. An admin checks your ID photo against the ID number and department records, then approves the account — you'll get a notification either way.

This keeps department ticket views limited to actual faculty, and stops anyone from posing as a teacher to see other departments' reports.
